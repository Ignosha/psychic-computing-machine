# Outreach: who buys this and how to reach them

Paid customers almost always come from direct contact, not passive listings. Aim for 20 conversations before tuning
anything.

## Best-fit buyers
1. **XRPL wallets and wallet SDK builders**: Pre-flight on every send; Token Risk before showing a token.
2. **Merchants and payment processors accepting XRP/RLUSD**: Settlement Verify before crediting.
3. **Exchanges, OTC desks and custodians with XRPL deposits**: Settlement Verify + sanctions on deposit addresses.
4. **DEX front-ends, token launchpads, NFT/token marketplaces on XRPL**: Token Risk grades on listings.
5. **Fintechs and crypto businesses generally**: sanctions and wallet screening with monitoring.

## Where to find them
- XRPL ecosystem directories and showcases, grant recipient lists, and projects announcing XRPL/RLUSD support.
- GitHub repositories using xrpl.js / xrpl-py (their maintainers are your developer buyers).
- Conferences and Discords/Telegrams in the XRPL community (respect each community's promotion rules).
